# Mini Brand Brief — Telecom AI Support

## Target Audience

The target audience consists of telecommunications and internet users in Palestine, especially young people, students, and families who are looking for quick and clear information about packages, services, and pricing.

It also includes users who prefer fast digital communication instead of browsing long pages or contacting customer service directly.

## Content Goal

This content package aims to:

- Introduce the audience to available internet services and packages.
- Present information in a simple, fast, and easy-to-understand way.
- Highlight the role of the AI assistant in answering customer inquiries.
- Encourage users to communicate with the assistant via Telegram.
- Deliver a modern visual experience inspired by the telecommunications industry.

## Tone of Voice

The tone should be:

- Simple and clear.
- Friendly and approachable.
- Direct and uncomplicated.
- Modern and tech-focused.
- Based on short, easy-to-read phrases.

Examples of the communication style:

- "Internet that fits your day… wherever you are!"
- "Packages that match your usage."
- "Got a question? Your answer is closer than you think!"
- "Ready to choose your package?"

## Visual Direction

The visual identity should follow a modern, energetic style inspired by the telecommunications industry.

### Colors

- Vibrant Green: `#76BC21`
- Shades of blue.
- Turquoise.
- White.
- Light Gray: `#F5F5F7`

### Visual Style

- Blue and turquoise gradient backgrounds.
- Green accents to highlight offers, prices, and key information.
- 3D elements and illustrations.
- Smartphones and digital communication elements.
- Wi-Fi, Telegram, and connectivity icons.
- Subtle neon lighting effects.
- Rounded cards and interface elements.
- Large, prominent numbers for package sizes, prices, and speeds, with supporting details shown in smaller text.

### Typography

Use a modern and highly readable Arabic typeface such as:

- Neo Sans Arabic
- Or a similar font available in the design platform.

Headlines should be bold and prominent, while secondary details should use smaller text without compromising readability.
