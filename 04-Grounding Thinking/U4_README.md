# U4 — GROUND ITS KNOWLEDGE

## Grounding Thinking

> A useful AI response is not enough. It should be supported by relevant, trustworthy information.

In this unit, I move from evaluating whether a solution works to examining **what its answers are based on**.

I explore how to connect AI-generated responses to reliable sources, retrieve relevant context, recognize unsupported claims, and manage situations where the available knowledge is incomplete.

The goal is to build solutions that do not simply sound convincing, but provide answers grounded in verifiable information.

---

## 🎯 Capability

**Grounding Thinking**

The ability to design and assess AI systems that use relevant knowledge sources, maintain a clear connection between evidence and generated answers, and acknowledge uncertainty when supporting information is unavailable.

---

## 🧠 What I Learned

- **Knowledge Sources:** Identify appropriate information sources and assess their relevance and reliability.
- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG):** Understand how retrieved context can support AI-generated responses.
- **Context Selection:** Provide the model with information that is relevant to the user's question.
- **Grounded Responses:** Check whether an answer is supported by the available source material.
- **Hallucination Awareness:** Recognize answers that introduce claims not supported by the evidence.
- **Knowledge Gaps:** Handle questions that cannot be answered confidently from the available information.
- **Traceability:** Keep the relationship between source content and generated outputs clear enough to review.

## 🔍 Engineering Focus

1. What information should the system rely on when answering a question?
2. How can I determine whether a knowledge source is relevant and trustworthy?
3. How does the system select the context needed for a specific request?
4. Can each important claim in the response be supported by the available evidence?
5. What should the system do when the source material is missing, incomplete, or conflicting?
6. How can I evaluate whether grounding changes improve response reliability?

---

## 📌 Key Takeaway

**A confident answer is not necessarily a correct answer. Reliable AI systems need relevant evidence and a clear way to handle uncertainty.**

Grounding helps shift AI development from generating plausible responses to producing answers that can be checked against known information.
